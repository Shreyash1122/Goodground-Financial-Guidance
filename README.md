# Goodground

A responsive, multipage financial guidance website for middle-class families in India. It includes browser-only monthly and investing-learning calculators, an illustrative savings projection, family goal planning, plain-language money guides, and educational FAQs.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No build step or account is required. The site has five pages:

- `index.html` — introduction and overview
- `planner.html` — live monthly budget snapshot and illustrative savings projection
- `goals.html` — emergency savings, home, education, retirement, debt and protection goals
- `guides.html` — plain-language household finance guides
- `investing.html` — salary and budget snapshot with broad investment topics to research, not stock or fund recommendations

## Notes

- Budget entries are calculated in the browser and are not sent to a server.
- The investing-learning calculator does not save or transmit entered numbers and does not name investments or give buy/sell instructions.
- The projection is an illustration using an assumed 8% annual growth rate. Returns are not guaranteed.
- Goodground provides general educational information, not personalized financial, investment, tax, legal, or insurance advice.
