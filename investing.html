How this tool thinks

  1. It estimates what remains after the costs you enter.
  2. It compares accessible emergency savings with the buffer goal you select.
  3. It points to broad categories and questions to research—not specific securities.

Your numbers may leave out irregular expenses, dependents, insurance, taxes or other goals. The result is only as complete as the information you enter.
